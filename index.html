About Our Oceans

A World Larger Than We Imagine

Oceans are home to an incredible variety of life, from microscopic organisms to the largest animal ever known. They regulate climate, produce oxygen, provide food and connect communities around the world.

Beneath the surface are coral reefs, kelp forests, underwater mountains, deep trenches and ecosystems that scientists are still discovering.

Oxygen

Marine organisms contribute significantly to the oxygen cycle.

Climate

Oceans absorb and distribute enormous amounts of heat.

Journey Through Ocean Zones

The ocean is divided into different zones based on depth, sunlight, temperature and pressure. Each zone contains unique forms of life.

01

Sunlight Zone

The brightest ocean layer where photosynthesis can occur.

02

Twilight Zone

A dim region where sunlight becomes extremely limited.

03

Midnight Zone

A dark world where sunlight does not reach.

04

Abyssal Zone

Cold, dark waters with immense pressure and unusual organisms.

05

Hadal Zone

The deepest ocean environments found within trenches.
